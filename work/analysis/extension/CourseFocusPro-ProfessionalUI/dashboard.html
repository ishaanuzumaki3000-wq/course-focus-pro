About

Course Focus Pro

A focused Chrome extension for students who want fewer distractions, clearer study routines, helpful analytics, music, hydration reminders, and steady progress without clutter.

Purpose

Why We Built Course Focus Pro

Course Focus Pro was built to help students stay consistent, reduce distracting detours, and turn daily effort into visible progress.

Privacy

Privacy Policy

Your study data, courses, missions, music metadata, and settings are stored locally in your browser. Local music files are not uploaded.
